Live Keys compact three-velocity edition v1: 16 sampled roots, original dynamic
layers v4, v9 and v15, full untrimmed decay. MP3 bytes remain unchanged.
At runtime this edition is decoded at 22050 Hz and averaged to mono for a bounded
PCM footprint, with each mono buffer peak-normalized to 0.7. New key/velocity mapping and performance envelopes are supplied
by Live Keys. The three Grand presets are tonal variations of this ONE bank.
No endorsement is implied. Retain attribution and license, including in mobile
redistribution. No additional restrictions are imposed on these recordings.
Audition and hardware acceptance are pending; compact edition is not a claim of
full original Salamander fidelity or a final approved release library.
